Our Story

Decorating Extraordinary Events Since 2015

We are a team of passionate event decorators and stylists who believe a well-dressed space transforms not just a room — but an entire experience. From the first fabric swatch to the last centrepiece, we make sure every detail is intentional, impactful, and unforgettable.

Who We Are

Crown House of Decor was founded on one belief: that every event deserves a space that matches the size of the occasion. We began as a boutique decor studio in Nigeria, and have since grown into a full-service event decoration and planning house trusted across Abuja, Lagos, and beyond.

We handle every element of your event's visual experience — from theme development and floral design to draping, lighting design, stage builds, and table styling. When you hire us, the look and feel of your event is entirely in our hands.

Our job is to make you walk into your own event and feel something. We plan so thoroughly that nothing surprises you on the day — except how beautiful it looks.

Creative Vision

We don't recycle themes. Every event gets a concept built specifically around your brief — your colours, your story, your aesthetic. The result is a space that feels entirely yours.

Flawless Delivery

On-time installation, zero cutting corners, and a team that doesn't leave until the space is exactly right. We hold ourselves to a standard your guests will notice — and remember.

Your Event, Your Way

We plan around your vision, not ours. Your brief drives every decision — from the colour palette to the centrepiece height. We're decorators first, and we never lose sight of who this event is for.
