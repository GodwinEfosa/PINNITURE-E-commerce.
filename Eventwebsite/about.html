Our Story

Planning Extraordinary Events Since 2012

We are a team of dedicated event planners and coordinators who believe every celebration deserves to run perfectly — not just look it. From the first consultation to the final farewell, we manage every detail so nothing is left to chance.

Who We Are

Founded over a decade ago, Arami Lux Events has grown from a boutique planning firm into one of Nigeria's most trusted event management companies. Our portfolio spans luxury weddings, corporate conferences, traditional ceremonies, and milestone celebrations — each one managed end to end.

We don't just show up on the day. We're with you from the first budget conversation to the final vendor invoice, making sure every supplier delivers, every timeline holds, and every guest has a seamless experience.

Our philosophy is simple: you should enjoy your own event. Whether it's an intimate gathering of 50 or a grand occasion for 500, we carry the operational weight so you can be fully present — not distracted by logistics.

Precision

We run on schedules, checklists, and contingency plans. Every timeline we build accounts for the unexpected, because flawless execution requires preparation, not luck.

Accountability

When you hire us, we own the outcome. We liaise with every vendor, confirm every booking, and stay on-site until the last guest leaves — no passing the buck.

Client-First

We don't plan events for portfolios. We plan them for you. Your brief, your budget, and your vision are the only brief that matters — and we protect all three.
