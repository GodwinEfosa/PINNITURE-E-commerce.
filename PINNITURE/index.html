  • Intentional delivery

Refund Policy

While refunds are not part of our policy, your satisfaction remains a priority.
We actively ensure:

  • Alignment before production begins

  • Professional handling of concerns
Our goal is simple: to deliver exactly what you envisioned—or better.

We offer exchange (not refunds) for eligible items under the following conditions:

IMPORTANT NOTE

  • All exchange requests are subject to inspection and approval
  • Items that do not meet our return standards will not be accepted

6. DELIVERY & ACCEPTANCE

We advise all clients to:

  • Inspect items immediately upon delivery
